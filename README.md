# Pablo Fernandez-Stearns

### Supply Chain Leadership · Planning · Data & Technology

[Career & selected work](https://stearns.digital/pablo/) · [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/pablofernandezstearns/)

I'm an Industrial Engineer with 20+ years in supply chain and operations, including 15 years in women's apparel at Juana Bonita, where I progressed from Production Planner to Manufacturing Manager and Head of Planning.

My experience spans demand forecasting, production and inventory planning, S&OP, budgeting and fulfillment. As Manufacturing Manager, I led 10 direct reports and an operation of 120 operators. I also built custom MRP, MPS and capacity-planning applications to connect operational decisions with execution.

Subsequent roles at Nuzest USA and PuroAir extended that experience into e-commerce operations, overseas purchasing and 3PL coordination. Today, as a Data Engineer at Rooms To Go, I develop a supply chain data warehouse, SQL/Python workflows in Databricks and self-service analytics with Genie.
